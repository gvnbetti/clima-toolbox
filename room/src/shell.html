Future climate
Global warming since 1850-1900. Approximate years: +1.5 °C around 2030, +2 °C around 2050, +3 °C around 2080 to 2100 (earlier on high emissions).
Urban context
Weather files usually come from airports outside the city. An urban context raises the air temperature by an estimated heat island (Theeuwes et al. 2017). Same model, presets and code as Macro Clima and Micro Clima; the preset values are placeholders.
Use
DIN 4108-2 boundary conditions for gains, schedules, base air change and heating setpoint.
Blind properties FC: share of solar heat let through when down; empty = DIN 4108-2 Table 7 by glazing (exterior 0.25, 0.30 with solar control glass; interior 0.70 double, 0.65 triple). Light: share of daylight let through when down (assumptions 0.10 and 0.25).

Comfort standard
When cooling is used, the room is held at the standard's upper limit in occupied hours: with the German annex the upper edge of its band; with EN 16798-1 and ASHRAE 55 their fixed limits for cooled rooms; with DIN 4108-2 the reference temperature of the region.
