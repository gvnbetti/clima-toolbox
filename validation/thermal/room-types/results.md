# Room types: the app against EnergyPlus

Generated by `analyse.py`. Calibration climates: the six weather files bundled in the app, in which Room Clima's engine settings were chosen (24 October 2026). Held-out climates: eight climates of the engine study that played no part in that choice, run with the same rooms and designs.

Free-running hourly operative temperature, app minus EnergyPlus: RMSE, mean bias and difference of the annual maximum, in K. Occupied degree-hours above 26 °C and heating need: app against EnergyPlus as a deviation of the total, with the EnergyPlus mean per room. Groups are by climate, use and room position only: the six designs per group cover every option level but not every combination, so splits by a single design option would mix the effects of several options.
