Clima Toolbox

Bringing climate into architectural design.

Clima Toolbox is a collection of free, open-source tools for exploring the relationship between climate, place and architecture.

Start by finding a weather file for your location. Understand the climate and explore future and urban climate scenarios, and set it beside a climate you know. Analyse outdoor comfort and test different configurations. Then move indoors to explore how room dimensions, facade design and building configurations influence energy, thermal comfort and daylight. When the work moves on to other simulation tools, take the future and urban climate with you in the weather file itself.

All tools run directly in your browser.

Clima Map

Finds the weather file.

Some 30,000 weather stations with 67,000 typical-year files. Search a place or click the map; the nearest stations come with their elevation and climate type, and each file downloads in one click.

  • Typical years from recent periods
  • Nearest stations to any point
  • Climate type of each station
Open Clima Map →

Clima Morph

Exports the weather file.

The future climate and the urban heat island of the toolbox, written into the weather file itself. The download is a valid EPW file for EnergyPlus, Ladybug and other simulation tools, with the same hours as in Macro, Micro and Room Clima.

  • +1.5, +2 and +3 °C of global warming
  • Urban heat island
  • Read by EnergyPlus and Ladybug
Open Clima Morph →

Clima Compare

Compares two weather files.

Two climates side by side, on the same scales: two places, two periods of one place, or a place today against a level of global warming. A written reading says what carries over from the climate you know, and what does not.

  • Every chart for both files
  • Solar time and season shift
  • Which strategies carry over
Open Clima Compare →

Macro Clima

Reads the weather file.

What the climate asks of a building: temperature and humidity through the year, sun, wind, sky, rain, comfort and the strategies that suit it, with the climate at +1.5, +2 and +3 °C of global warming and the urban heat island.

  • Comfort, sun, wind, rain
  • Future climate after IPCC AR6
  • Urban heat island
Open Macro Clima →

Micro Clima

Outdoor comfort.

Which moves make an outdoor space comfortable, and for how many hours: trees, canopies, ground surfaces, walls, courtyards and street canyons, compared hour by hour with the thermal comfort index UTCI.

  • Shade, ground, wind, enclosure
  • Hours of comfort gained or lost
  • Season by season trade-offs
Open Micro Clima →

Room Clima

Room and facade.

What to build, facade by facade: window size, glazing, shading, ventilation, mass and fans searched against daylight, overheating, energy and whole-life carbon, with the DIN 4108-2 summer check.

  • Hourly ISO 52016-1 room model
  • Checked against EnergyPlus and Radiance
  • Packages that meet every target
Open Room Clima →

Checked

Every model is checked against an established tool: the room model against EnergyPlus and ASHRAE Standard 140, daylight against Radiance, outdoor comfort against Ladybug, and the climate calculations against pvlib, pythermalcomfort and the IPCC Atlas data. See the validation page for the results and their limits.

Weather

Typical-year files from Climate.OneBuilding.org (Lawrie and Crawley) and the EnergyPlus collection. Future climate from the IPCC AR6 Atlas. Your own EPW files work in every app and never leave your computer.
