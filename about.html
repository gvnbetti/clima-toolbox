About Clima Toolbox

Clima Toolbox brings together five free, open-source browser tools for climate-informed architectural design. The tools follow a simple sequence: find the right weather data, understand the climate, explore outdoor comfort, and investigate the performance of rooms and facades. Clima Morph carries the future and urban climate into a weather file for other simulation tools.

The aim is to make climate analysis more accessible to architects and designers, and to connect environmental understanding with early design decisions. Each tool is accompanied by a guide explaining its methods, assumptions and possible applications.

Who is behind it

Clima Toolbox is developed by Prof. Giovanni Betti (LinkedIn), an architect and researcher based in Berlin, Germany.

He is Professor of Building Technology and Building Physics at the German International University in Berlin and Strategic Advisor for Sustainability and Innovation at David Chipperfield Architects Berlin.

Previously, he was Associate Partner in the Specialist Modelling Group at Foster + Partners, where he worked on computational design and building performance; founded the Performance-Based Design team at HENN; served as Assistant Professor at the Center for the Built Environment (CBE), UC Berkeley; and held a Guest Professorship in Digital Experimental Design at UdK Berlin.

At UC Berkeley, he co-developed the CBE Clima Tool with Federico Tartarini, Christine Nguyen, Stefano Schiavon and many others (see contributors). Clima Toolbox builds on that experience but is an independent project, with its own code, models and methods.

Clima Toolbox is a personal project. It is not developed on behalf of, or endorsed by, the German International University, David Chipperfield Architects or any other institution mentioned above.

How it is made

Clima Toolbox is developed with the assistance of Claude, Anthropic's AI model, under Giovanni Betti's direction. The design questions, methods, assumptions and performance criteria are defined and reviewed by the author. AI assists with coding and development, while responsibility for the underlying methods and their validation remains with the author.

Models are checked against established simulation tools and reference methods. For example, Room Clima's hourly calculations are compared with EnergyPlus, and its daylight calculations with Radiance. Validation scripts, comparisons and results are publicly available in the GitHub repository.

The tools run directly in your browser. Weather files loaded by users remain on their own computers.

Use and citation

All code is freely available under the MIT licence. Documentation and validation materials are published under CC BY 4.0.

You are welcome to use, adapt and build on the tools. If you use them in teaching, practice or research, please provide attribution using the reference in the repository's CITATION.cff.

Clima Toolbox is an ongoing project. The tools are continuously being developed, tested and refined. They are intended for early-stage design exploration and education, not as a substitute for detailed building simulation or professional verification. Always check results before using them for critical design decisions. The tools are provided without warranty.
